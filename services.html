Hotel Services

Aurora Hotel offers a range of services designed to make every stay comfortable, relaxing and convenient.

>

Restaurant

The Aurora Hotel restaurant offers breakfast, lunch and dinner for hotel guests. The menu includes local and international dishes, as well as lighter and vegetarian options.

  • Daily breakfast buffet for hotel guests
  • Local and international cuisine
  • Vegetarian and children's menu options
  • Hot drinks, desserts and snacks during the day
  • Allergen information available on request

Spa & Wellness

Our Spa & Wellness area is designed for guests who want to relax and recover during their stay. Massage and beauty treatments can be booked through the hotel reception.

  • Massage and relaxation treatments
  • Sauna and quiet relaxation area
  • Towels and changing facilities provided
  • Beauty and wellness treatments by appointment
  • Advance reservation is recommended

Swimming Pool

The indoor heated swimming pool is available for hotel guests who would like to relax or exercise during their stay.

  • Indoor heated swimming pool
  • Free access for hotel guests
  • Fresh towels provided
  • Changing rooms and showers available
  • Children must be supervised by an adult

Fitness Center

The fitness center provides equipment for both cardio and strength training. Guests can continue their regular exercise routine while travelling.

  • Treadmills and exercise bikes
  • Free weights and strength equipment
  • Free access for registered hotel guests
  • Water station inside the fitness area
  • Changing facilities available nearby

Room Service

Guests can order selected meals, drinks and snacks directly to their room. Ordering information is available in the room or through hotel reception.

  • Meals and drinks delivered to the room
  • Breakfast in the room available on request
  • Selection of snacks and hot drinks
  • Orders can be placed through reception
  • Additional charges may apply

Free Wi-Fi

High-speed wireless internet is available throughout Aurora Hotel, including guest rooms and public areas, without an additional charge.

  • Available in every guest room
  • Available in the lobby and restaurant
  • No additional charge for hotel guests
  • Suitable for video calls and streaming
  • Reception can help with connection problems
